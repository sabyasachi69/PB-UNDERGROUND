# Demo imagery provenance

The 40 catalogue styles each have an original AI concept photograph at `public/images/demo/<product-slug>.webp`. These are illustrations of proposed garments, not photographs of real PB UNDERGROUND inventory, suppliers or customers. Product cards and galleries label them as AI concepts. The first listed colour is the colour shown in the image. Alternate colour swatches are sample catalogue options, not photographed variants. The second gallery view is a closer crop of the same image, not a separate detail photograph.

The first 12 product concepts were generated on 28 October 2026. On 31 October 2026, 28 new concepts were generated to bring each of Layers, Shirts, Bottoms and Essentials to ten styles. Five earlier photographs were also replaced with garment-focused studio compositions: Gate Seven Hoodie, Lecture Hall Overshirt, 2AM Cargo, Everyday Heavy Tee and Canteen Cap. The other seven existing concepts remain in use.

The 28 new styles and their garment, primary colour and photographic framing are recorded in [`src/data/catalogue-expansion.json`](../src/data/catalogue-expansion.json). Each generated shot used a distinct adult model or crop, neutral warm-grey studio setting, soft key and fill light, restrained rim light, sharp fabric detail, no logos or text, and framing that gives the garment visual priority. Bottoms show the whole leg; shirts and layers show the front and construction; small essentials occupy most of the frame. The replacement shots follow the same direction. The approved PNG outputs were converted to WebP at quality 86; the 40 delivered assets total about 4 MB. Next.js Image serves responsive sizes.

The user-provided `Fashion_Model_Reference_Pack_120_Photos.zip` supplied category and commercial-photography art direction. Its README identifies the photographs as copyrighted retailer images without verified rights for AI input or redistribution. None of its photos was copied into the repository or sent to the image generator. The new concepts were generated from written garment descriptions and lighting/framing direction.

Before accepting live orders, replace the AI concepts with owner-approved photographs of the actual garments in every offered colour, verify the inventory and measurements, and confirm the final product claims with suppliers.
