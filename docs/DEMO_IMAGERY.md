All prompts specified an adult model, realistic fabric texture, no institution signage, no other people, no watermark and no invented brand marks. The approved outputs were converted from PNG to WebP at quality 84 for efficient delivery. The originals remain in the generation workspace; the WebP assets above are the project-bound copies. Next.js Image serves responsive sizes. Replace these concepts with owner-approved photos before accepting live orders.

The supplied `Fashion_Model_Reference_Pack_120_Photos.zip` was consulted for its written commercial photography direction: neutral backgrounds, soft studio lighting and visible garment construction. Its README identifies the pictures as copyrighted retailer photographs without verified AI-input or redistribution rights, so none of those JPEGs was copied to this repository or provided to the image generator. The eight new studio prompts use the user's lighting, lens, framing and clothing-focus guidance rather than a specific retailer image.

Motion added with the imagery: a small image scale on hover for precise pointers, slow ambient movement in the hero graphic, and progressive scroll entrance for selected editorial headings and product cards where `animation-timeline: view()` is supported. All of these effects are optional decoration and are suppressed by `prefers-reduced-motion`.
