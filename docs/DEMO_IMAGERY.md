All prompts specified an adult model, realistic fabric texture, restrained red/black/bone art direction, no institution signage, no other people, no watermark and no invented brand marks. The approved outputs were converted from PNG to WebP at quality 84 for efficient delivery. The four originals remain in the generation workspace; the WebP assets above are the project-bound copies. Next.js Image serves responsive sizes. Replace these concepts with owner-approved photos before accepting live orders.

Motion added with the imagery: a small image scale on hover for precise pointers, slow ambient movement in the hero graphic, and progressive scroll entrance for selected editorial headings and product cards where `animation-timeline: view()` is supported. All of these effects are optional decoration and are suppressed by `prefers-reduced-motion`.
